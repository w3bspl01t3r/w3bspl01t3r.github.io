Delivery flow

Predictable process. Adaptive testing.

The administrative parts stay clear so technical time can go toward finding and validating the risk.

01

Discovery & scoping

Confirm business context, assets, exclusions, accounts, environments, data-handling expectations, and success criteria.

02

Rules of engagement

Agree testing windows, escalation contacts, prohibited actions, evidence handling, and stop conditions before activity begins.

03

Assessment

Run manual and tool-assisted testing, communicate critical discoveries early, and adapt effort as attack paths emerge.

04

Report & readout

Deliver prioritized findings and attack-path context, then work through the results with technical and business owners.

05

Remediation & retest

Answer fix questions, evaluate compensating controls, and verify remediation when included in the engagement.

Operating principles

Safe testing is part of good testing.

The exact controls are agreed per engagement, but authorization and operational safety are never implied.

Written authorization

Assets, environments, testing methods, and accountable contacts are confirmed before work begins.

Minimum necessary impact

Findings are proven with the least disruptive method that establishes the risk within scope.

Critical escalation

Severe or actively dangerous conditions are communicated through the agreed channel without waiting for the final report.

Controlled evidence

Sensitive evidence is minimized, handled according to the agreed engagement terms, and never published as portfolio material.
