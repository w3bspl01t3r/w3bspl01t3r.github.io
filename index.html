How we work

A clear path from scope to safer systems.

Testing is only useful when your team understands what is exposed, why it matters, and exactly what to do next.

01

Model the risk

Define critical assets, trust boundaries, likely adversaries, constraints, and the outcomes that would hurt most.

02

Test like an attacker

Combine manual analysis, targeted tooling, code knowledge, and exploit-chain research to uncover meaningful paths.

03

Prove impact safely

Validate findings within agreed rules of engagement, minimize operational risk, and capture evidence your team can trust.

04

Fix and verify

Deliver prioritized remediation guidance, walk engineers through root causes, and retest fixes where included.
