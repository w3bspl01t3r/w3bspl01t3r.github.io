Coordinated disclosure

Vulnerability disclosure policy.

A transparent process for reporting security issues to ZDayLabs and for publishing vulnerabilities discovered through independent research.

ZDayLabs supports coordinated disclosure that gives affected maintainers a reasonable opportunity to investigate and remediate while preserving the public interest in timely security information.

Reporting an issue to ZDayLabs

Email research@zdaylabs.com with a clear description of the issue.

Please include

  • The affected URL, page, or repository
  • Reproduction steps and required preconditions
  • Observed and potential impact
  • Supporting requests, responses, screenshots, or proof when safe
  • A secure way to contact you if ordinary email is not appropriate
Do not include unnecessary personal data, credentials, production secrets, or data belonging to other people.

Good-faith research expectations

  • Test only what is necessary to demonstrate the issue.
  • Do not degrade availability, destroy data, establish persistence, or pivot to unrelated systems.
  • Stop if you encounter sensitive or personal data and report the exposure without retaining it.
  • Give ZDayLabs a reasonable opportunity to investigate before publishing details.
  • Do not use a vulnerability to demand payment or other action.

Our independent research process

Standard disclosure window

ZDayLabs generally works on a 90-day disclosure window from initial vendor notification. The window may be adjusted through coordination when a complex fix requires reasonable additional time.

Early disclosure conditions

Details may be published earlier when active exploitation is evident, users face immediate severe risk, the vendor is persistently unresponsive, or public disclosure clearly serves the broader security interest.

Information shared with vendors

  • Technical description and affected behavior
  • Reproduction or proof-of-concept material when appropriate
  • Impact and exploitability assessment
  • Suggested remediation direction
  • Proposed disclosure timeline

Publication

After coordination, a public write-up may include technical analysis, remediation guidance, a communication timeline, and proof-of-concept detail appropriate to the risk and patch availability.

Public research is intended to help defenders understand and remove the vulnerable pattern - not expose customer engagement data or confidential systems.

Contact

Questions, vulnerability reports, and disclosure coordination can be sent to research@zdaylabs.com. A PGP key can be coordinated when needed.
