Research practice

From root cause to practical impact.

The work goes beyond identifying a vulnerable endpoint. It traces the implementation, establishes preconditions, develops safe proof, and documents how to reduce the risk.

Implementation analysis

Follow data and control flow through framework, library, and application boundaries.

Exploit-chain development

Test whether isolated weaknesses combine into a credible path with meaningful impact.

Coordinated disclosure

Share reproducible detail with maintainers and publish according to the disclosure policy.
